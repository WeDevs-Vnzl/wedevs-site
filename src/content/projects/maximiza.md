  context: >-
    **Maximiza Casa de Bolsa** is a brokerage whose web presence ran on an aging
    stack that limited its SEO reach and gave the business **no visibility into the
    leads** generated by its own site. The project covers a full modernization:
    rebuild the public site on a current framework, fix the SEO foundation, and
    give the team an **internal backoffice with a CRM** to capture and monitor
    every prospect who starts the multistep onboarding form or reaches out over
    **WhatsApp**.

  conditions: >-
    The public site and the admin panel are **separate Nuxt 4 apps** (SSR for SEO,
    SPA for the internal tool) talking to **a shared NestJS backend** on serverless
    Neon Postgres. Content (blog, financial balances) is served **over REST from
    Kairos CMS**, while all business logic, auth, and lead tracking live in the
    NestJS service. Sessions use a **JWT in an httpOnly cookie**, and lead updates
    must reach the CRM **live**, not on refresh.

  solution: >-
    The platform is split into **three apps around one backend**. The public site
    is **Nuxt 4 SSR** with a rebuilt SEO layer (sitemap, robots, structured
    metadata) and a **PWA**; its **multistep onboarding form** posts each step to
    the backend so no lead is lost mid-flow. The internal **backoffice is a Nuxt 4
    SPA** (@nuxt/ui + Pinia) whose **CRM module** lists, assigns, and monitors
    those leads, with **ApexCharts** dashboards and **live updates over
    Socket.IO**. The backend is **NestJS 11 + Drizzle ORM on Neon Postgres**,
    exposing a consistent response envelope and cookie-based JWT auth; it also
    runs a **multichannel chatbot** as a finite-state machine sharing the same
    data layer. Onboarding PDFs are generated in-process with Puppeteer.
